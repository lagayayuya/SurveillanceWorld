# Decisions

One line per decision: date — decision — reason.

- 2026-09-27 — v1 scope is "see" only; exposure per street segment is kept as the future routing cost — roadmap order: see, avoid, survey.
- 2026-09-27 — Code under AGPL-3.0-or-later, derived data under ODbL 1.0 — requested licences; ODbL is required by OSM-derived databases.
- 2026-09-27 — Python pipeline (uv, pyosmium, shapely 2, h3) writing GeoParquet, no database — reproducible one-command batch per region, nothing to operate.
- 2026-09-27 — Exact viewshed (rays to building corners ± epsilon, cone edges, sampled range arc), no fixed angular step — accuracy is a first-rank criterion.
- 2026-09-27 — Tiles built with tippecanoe into PMTiles, served statically over HTTP Range, rendered with MapLibre GL JS — cheapest self-hostable stack, no tile server.
- 2026-09-27 — Self-hosted Protomaps basemap, grey flavour — ODbL, no third-party service, desaturated background. → complétée le 2026-10-05 : l'interface punk a son propre fond (« The punk basemap is ours… »)
- 2026-09-27 — H3 grid (res 7 and 8) for the city view, H3 res 6 as the processing unit — stable hierarchical ids, parallel and incremental processing later. → remplacée le 2026-09-29 (« Hexagons removed… »)
- 2026-09-27 — Four operator classes (public / private outdoor / private indoor / unknown); "films the public street" is a computed badge — OSM tags do not say who films for most cameras.
- 2026-09-27 — Feasibility test = two neighbouring suburban towns, frozen geometric cases, and all of Île-de-France — the two towns have 62 cameras and none with a direction.
- 2026-09-27 — Python 3.13 locally, >=3.12 supported — 3.13 is the installed interpreter.
- 2026-09-27 — Buildings are prepared once per cluster (connected at < 1 m), not per block — per-block union + closing moved vertices by float noise and broke the "same result with or without blocks" property.
- 2026-09-27 — Wall cameras are snapped 0.5 m out of their building, at most 10 m deep; deeper = data error, no view — 5,234 IDF cameras are drawn on or in their building; 86 are more than 10 m inside.
- 2026-09-27 — Thin view parts are classified as leaks or grazing wedges; the user's spike metric is kept as is — all 199 IDF spikes (50 m ranges) are legitimate wedges or sight lines. Leak definition superseded below. → remplacée le 2026-09-27 (« Leak = thin view part… »)
- 2026-09-27 — The viewshed QA stage fails the build on a property violation or an uncut view, not on spikes — spikes are a go/no-go metric, violations are bugs.
- 2026-09-27 — CI fixture = a 600 m box at La Défense (75 KB) — densest cell of directional cameras in IDF, with streets under the slab.
- 2026-09-27 — MapLibre 6 worker imported with `?worker&url` — required by MapLibre's bundler docs; the map stays blank otherwise.
- 2026-09-27 — Step 1 zoom scheme: hexagons (H3 res 7) to z11, filmed street segments from z11, all segments from z14, sidewalks/crossings from z16, filmed areas and pictograms from z15 — user decision on readability. → en partie remplacée le 2026-09-28 (« City view: départements z5-8… »)
- 2026-09-27 — One hue for everything surveillance-related; operator only in filters and the sheet — user decision; shape still tells the type.
- 2026-09-27 — Two precomputed aggregate sets (all cameras / public only), switched by the "who films" filter below z15 — filters cannot recompute street shares client-side.
- 2026-09-27 — Hexagon colours by quintiles over cells with a camera, breaks in tiles/meta.json — shares are very skewed (IDF median ~1 %, max 19 %). → remplacée le 2026-09-29 (« Hexagons removed… »)
- 2026-09-27 — Viewshed QA blocks the build on any leak; grazing wedges accepted — user decision after the feasibility review.
- 2026-09-27 — CNIL signal hidden (VITE_CNIL_SIGNAL=false) until 20 random cases are reviewed — user decision. → remplacée le 2026-09-27 (« CNIL signal enabled in the private preview… »)
- 2026-09-27 — Observation levels follow IEC 62676-4 DORI in PPM (25 / 62.5 / 125 / 250); four stored, three drawn (>= 25 PPM shown as "observation") — user decision; 62.5 to verify on the text of the standard. → nom du troisième niveau précisé le 2026-10-06 (« détecter »)
- 2026-09-27 — Uncertain optics = a (resolution, hfov) range per camera sampled on a 5 x 5 log grid; coverage bands p >= 1 / 0.75 / 0.5 / 0.25 / > 0 computed exactly in polar slices — reach and field of view move in opposite directions, so zones are not nested by sample and need a count, not a union.
- 2026-09-27 — Provisional market ranges in rules/optics.yaml, every camera at certainty "default" until the catalogue (step 2) — no OSM tag gives resolution or field of view.
- 2026-09-27 — Computation range capped at 150 m — the best default case exceeds 280 m at 25 PPM.
- 2026-09-27 — Exposure per level on the p >= 0.5 band, plus expected length (sum over bands) as the future routing cost; street colour at z15+ = highest level reached — user decision; expectation keeps uncertainty usable by a router.
- 2026-09-27 — camera:angle used as tilt only when 5-80 deg in magnitude and height is at least estimated — signs are used both ways in the data.
- 2026-09-27 — The view is simplified once at 5 cm before cutting the bands — exact views carry ~3 vertices per building corner; bands went from 1,054 to ~47 vertices on average and stay exactly nested.
- 2026-09-27 — Rays are bucketed by bearing (64 buckets) before segment tests — identical geometry on the feasibility towns (0 m2 difference), visibility 4x faster at 150 m.
- 2026-09-27 — QA overlap only intersects buildings the view enters by more than 1 cm; one opening for spikes and leaks — QA had become the largest cost with 150 m views.
- 2026-09-27 — Overlay operations fall back to a 1 mm precision grid on GEOS TopologyException — the IDF run crashed on a non-noded intersection.
- 2026-09-27 — Leak = thin view part lying (>= 0.25 m2) in the gap zone of two buildings less than 30 cm apart (adjoining buildings mapped with a sliver) — with 150 m ranges the "outline against buildings" and "any two buildings < 1 m" rules flagged real sight lines through passages and along facades.
- 2026-09-27 — Bands cut in camera-centred coordinates and stored on a 1 mm grid; nesting checked with snap-rounding overlays and 2 mm slack — at Lambert-93 coordinates GEOS silently returned an empty intersection for nested bands.
- 2026-09-27 — Leak criterion validated after manual review: a view leaks when it passes between two buildings less than 30 cm apart; remaining tapered corners and spikes are normal; 30 cm and 0.25 m2 stay [TO ADJUST] — user decision after review.
- 2026-09-27 — DORI levels follow IEC 62676-4:2014 (25 / 62.5 / 125 / 250 px/m), the edition is cited in code and UI; observation 62.5 confirmed by a secondary source (Axis white paper on IEC 62676-4:2014: 63 px/m, i.e. 10 px for a 16 cm face) — the 2025 edition cuts levels differently; 2014 keeps the displayed verbs clear.
- 2026-09-27 — Three levels displayed, no public/private distinction drawn on the map (operator only in filters and the sheet) — user decision.
- 2026-09-27 — CNIL signal enabled in the private preview build after review of the 20 cases; legal review of the wording required before any public release — user decision; supersedes "hidden until reviewed".
- 2026-09-27 — Three reach estimates replace the five bands: minimum certain (p >= 0.9), probable (p >= 0.5, default), cautious (p >= 0.1); the map draws the chosen one, a click shows the camera's three; exposure and cell shares stored per estimate x aggregate set — user decision; p > 0 dropped. → remplacée le 2026-09-28 puis le 2026-09-30 (« Two hypotheses on the map… », « The top bar offers two optical hypotheses… »)
- 2026-09-27 — PTZ: the filled field is the wide-angle one (all estimates apply); the zoomed "reachable range" is a thin outline cut by buildings, excluded from fill and street exposure, quoted in the sheet ("can be aimed at you up to ~X m"); the range cap applies to filled fields only — a zoomed PTZ aims at one direction at a time. → contour PTZ remplacé le 2026-09-30 (« The PTZ zoomed-reach outline (`ptz-reach`) leaves the map… »)
- 2026-09-27 — Advanced filters in a drawer, collapsed by default, apply to cameras at street zoom only, counts from z15; freshness = max(check_date, survey:date) in < 1 y / 1-3 y / > 3 y / never; OSM edit date shown apart as a weak signal — user decision.
- 2026-09-27 — All pictograms look the same by default; only "maybe gone" (fixme) is always pale; freshness rendering (pictos and hexagons: share verified < 3 y, quantile scale, grey under 3 cameras) only in the "show: data freshness" mode — 82 % of IDF cameras are never or > 3 y verified, so default paling would invert the signal. → remplacée les 2026-09-28 et 2026-09-29 (« The "show: data freshness" display… », « Pictograms are signed distance fields… »)
- 2026-09-27 — Normalised operator names in rules/operators.yaml, each with a class used (certainty "estimated") when tags say nothing about who films — 649 raw names on 3,244 IDF cameras.
- 2026-09-27 — Streets z11-13 are filmed axes merged by colour class (share in quarters, both aggregate sets) and simplified to 0.5 px; segments with their ids only from z14; unused edge attributes (n, a) dropped — heaviest z11 tile 481 -> 188 KiB, z12 210 -> 82 KiB, identical rendering.
- 2026-09-27 — CI smoke test runs on the La Défense extract with a versioned Protomaps basemap extract (2.9 MB, z0-15) in tests/fixtures — the map is exercised in a browser on every push without downloading the basemap. → précisée le 2026-10-06 (au push, le seul test du téléphone en WebKit punk)
- 2026-09-27 — Covered length of a segment = union of intervals of distance along it, not a 2D union of line pieces — pieces from different bands, snapped to 1 mm, are not exactly collinear and GEOS summed their overlaps.
- 2026-09-27 — Cell colour breaks come from the probable estimate and serve all three; street classes are absolute quarters — per-estimate quantiles would redraw the same ranks and hide the change when switching.
- 2026-09-27 — Each estimate's street length is measured on the union of its band and the more certain ones; QA fails the build if a street is more filmed with a more certain estimate — bands nest only up to the 1 mm grid and a street along a band edge fell in one and out of the next.
- 2026-09-27 — Advanced-filter attributes packed in one integer per camera (freshness bucket + one bit per property) plus operator and zone indices, copied on its bands and PTZ outline — a view is filtered with its camera even when the camera's tile is not loaded; detail z15 97 -> 100 KiB.
- 2026-09-27 — Operator class priority: operator:type, then the normalised-name table, then name patterns, then surveillance=* — surveillance=public says what is filmed, not who films; 39 IDF cameras reclassified, "unknown operator" unchanged (those cameras have no name at all).
- 2026-09-27 — Capabilities for filters come only from tags (camera:features tokens, ALPR, PTZ), listed in rules/capabilities.yaml; no IDF camera tags any image analytics, so that row is hidden until one does.
- 2026-09-27 — Camera catalogue in catalogue/models.csv (one row per model, manufacturer pages and datasheets only, release-year source recorded) and catalogue/tenders.csv (French municipal tenders readable without an account); optics left empty when not published — clean-room rule; rows without resolution and field of view do not enter the calibration.
- 2026-09-27 — rules/optics.yaml holds per type x generation p10/p50/p90 of resolution and horizontal field of view, generated by `lacarte calibrate` from catalogue/models.csv; a cell under 5 models uses the type over all generations, a type under 5 keeps its provisional range (doorbells) — sourced distributions replace uniform grids; every sparse cell is visible in the file.
- 2026-09-27 — Sampling: per generation, the 3 x 3 grid of tercile midpoints (1/6, 1/2, 5/6, log-normal interpolation between the percentiles), weighted by the installed-base mix 20/30/35/15 (hypothesis); resolution and field of view taken as independent (catalogue Spearman: dome 0.22, fixed 0.10, panorama 0.05; ALPR 0.45 and PTZ -0.49 on 13 and 9 models) — the percentiles stay the single source; the independence is flagged where it is doubtful.
- 2026-09-27 — Varifocal models count over their range (five log-spaced settings); PTZ fill their wide field, their tele end only sets the zoomed-reach outline (p90 resolution, p10 tele field) — how a lens is set on site is unknown.
- 2026-09-27 — Lenses wider than 150 deg use the equidistant (fisheye) model, image diameter at most 180 deg — the rectilinear formula gave negative reaches for 360-degree cameras.
- 2026-09-27 — Filled fields capped at 180 m (was 150): covers the cautious reach of every type but ALPR (dome 177 m); PTZ outline cap kept at 150 m pending a decision (measured: 300 m costs +8 % views time, 500 m +25 %). → partie contour PTZ remplacée le 2026-09-30 (« The PTZ zoomed-reach outline (`ptz-reach`)… »)
